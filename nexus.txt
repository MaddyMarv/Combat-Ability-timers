Standalone mod that adds a timer to your HUD that counts down the duration of your active ability. Sometimes it's hard to tell when your ability is going to run out in the middle of a horde.

[b]Options:[/b]

[b]Display[/b]
- Display Mode [Timer + Progress Bar, Progress Bar Only, Timer Only]
- Show Bracket & Bubble Health (%)
- Default Bar & Text Colors
- Use Progress Color Toggles (Bar, Text)
- High, Mid, Low Duration Colors
- Track Cooldown
- Cooldown Display Mode [Smooth (start from remaining), Full (show complete cooldown)]
- Cooldown Color
- Scrier's Gaze Bar (Use Gaze Bar, Static/Progress Peril Color, Low & High Peril Colors)

[b]Charges[/b]
- Show Ability Charges
- Always Show Charges (even at 1 or 0)

[b]Gauge[/b]
- Gauge Length & Thickness
- Gauge Opacity
- Orientation [Horizontal, Vertical]
- Bar Direction [Start, End, Center]

[b]Position[/b]
- X/Y Offset Options (Bar, Timer Text, Charges, Bubble Health)
- Text Alignment [Left, Center, Right]
- Text Size

[b]Filters[/b]
- Track Specific Abilities & Buffs (Voice of Command, Volley Fire, Scrier's Gaze, Point-Blank Barrage, Bull Rush, Chorus of Spiritual Fortitude, Fury of the Faithful, Shroudfield, Telekine Shield, etc...)
