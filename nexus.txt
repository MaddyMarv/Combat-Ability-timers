HUD countdown timer showing the active duration of your combat ability.

[b]Options:[/b]

[b]Display[/b]
- Show Ability Timer UI
- Display Mode [Timer + Progress Bar, Progress Bar Only, Timer Only]
- Always Show Gauge
- Show Ability Charges
- Always Show Charges (even at 1 or 0)
- Show Bracket & Decimals
- Cooldown Target Timer [Next Charge, Until Full]
- Show Bubble Shield Health & Display Mode [Both (Mirror Ability Bar), Both (Chronological), Lowest Health Only, Newest Bubble Only, Oldest Bubble Only]
- Enable Special Scrier's Gaze Bar

[b]Gauge[/b]
- Gauge Length & Thickness
- Gauge Opacity
- Orientation [Horizontal, Vertical]
- Bar Direction [Start, End, Center]
- Segmented Bar & Cooldown Target Bar [Until Full, Next Charge]
- Independent Charge Tracking & Fixed Charge Slots
- Consume Order [Right to Left, Left to Right] & Segment Spacing
- Bar Notches [None, 2, 3, 4, 6], Length & Width
- Show Cooldown on Gauge & Cooldown Display Mode [Smooth Animation, Full Duration]

[b]Colors[/b]
- Base Active Bar & Text Colors
- Use Progress Colors (Bar & Text)
- High (>66%), Mid (33-66%), Low (<33%) Duration Colors
- Ready Charge Segment & Bar Notch Divider Colors
- Static Cooldown Color & Progress Cooldown Colors (0% & 100%)
- Static Peril Color & Progress Peril Colors (<50% & >50%)

[b]Position[/b]
- X/Y Offset Options (Gauge, Timer Text, Charges, Bubble Health)
- Text Alignment [Left, Center, Right] (Timer Text, Charges, Bubble Health)
- Text Size (Timer Text, Charges, Bubble Health)

[b]Filters[/b]
- Track Specific Abilities & Buffs (Voice of Command, Volley Fire, Scrier's Gaze, Point-Blank Barrage, Bull Rush, Chorus of Spiritual Fortitude, Fury of the Faithful, Shroudfield, Telekine Shield, etc...)
